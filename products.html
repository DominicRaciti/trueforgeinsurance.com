Protect Your Home & Your Family's Income

Term life insurance is the most straightforward, affordable way to make sure your family keeps the house and keeps living their life if something happens to you. It covers a set period — 10, 15, 20, or 30 years — at a fixed monthly premium.

Mortgage Protection is a specialized term policy designed to pay off your remaining mortgage balance directly, so your family never has to worry about losing their home.

  • 10, 15, 20, and 30-year term options
  • Simplified issue — no medical exam needed
  • Level premiums that never increase
  • Mortgage payoff rider available
  • Return of premium options available
  • Convertible to permanent coverage
  • Same-day approval with some carriers
  • Available for ages 18–75
Ideal For

Homeowners, young families, income earners with dependents, or anyone with a mortgage they want paid off in the event of their death.

Indexed Universal Life

Life Insurance That Builds Real Wealth

An IUL is a permanent life insurance policy with a cash value component that grows based on the performance of a stock market index (like the S&P 500) — but with a 0% floor, meaning your money can never go backwards due to market losses.

Many clients use IULs as a tax-advantaged supplement to their retirement savings — pulling tax-free income in retirement while keeping their family protected for life.

  • Permanent life insurance — never expires
  • Cash value grows with market index performance
  • 0% floor — your cash never loses due to market drops
  • Tax-free withdrawals and loans from cash value
  • Flexible premiums — adjust as your life changes
  • Can supplement retirement income
  • Death benefit paid income-tax-free to beneficiaries
  • Living benefits for critical/chronic illness
Ideal For

Business owners, high earners who've maxed out their 401(k)/IRA, families wanting permanent coverage with a savings component, or anyone planning for tax-free retirement income.

Leave a Legacy — Not a Burden

Final expense insurance is a small whole life policy designed to cover funeral costs, medical bills, and other end-of-life expenses. The average funeral in America costs $9,000–$12,000 — and that doesn't include outstanding debts or medical costs.

Unlike term insurance, whole life never expires and builds cash value over time. Your premium stays locked in for life, and your benefit is guaranteed.

  • Permanent whole life — coverage never expires
  • No medical exam required
  • Guaranteed acceptance options for ages 50–85
  • Premiums locked in for life — never increase
  • Cash value accumulates over time
  • Benefit paid directly to named beneficiary
  • Covers funeral, burial, medical, and other costs
  • Fast approval — often within 24 hours
Ideal For

Seniors ages 50–85 who want to cover funeral and final costs without burdening their family. Even if you have diabetes, heart conditions, COPD, or have been declined before — we have options. Guaranteed acceptance means no health questions at all.

Get Covered Without the Waiting Room

Traditional life insurance can take weeks — medical exams, blood draws, urine samples, waiting on underwriting. Simplified issue skips all of that. You answer a short health questionnaire, and most carriers make a decision the same day.

This isn't a second-tier product. Many simplified issue policies from our top carriers offer the same benefits as fully underwritten policies at competitive rates.

  • No physical exam, no blood draw, no labs
  • Short health questionnaire only
  • Approval decisions in minutes to hours
  • Available for term, whole life, and IUL products
  • Ideal for individuals with mild health conditions
  • Competitive rates — often comparable to full underwriting
  • Multiple carrier options for best approval odds
  • Coverage can begin same day
Ideal For

Anyone who wants fast coverage, individuals with health conditions who've been declined elsewhere, or those who simply don't want to deal with the traditional underwriting process.
