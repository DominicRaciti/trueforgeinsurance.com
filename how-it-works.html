The Process

Simple. Fast. No Surprises.

Most clients go from first call to active policy in under a week — often the same day. Here's exactly how it works.

1
Step One

We Have a Real Conversation

You call me or schedule a free 15-minute consultation. No scripts, no high-pressure tactics. I'm going to ask you some straightforward questions:

  • What are you trying to protect? (Your family, your mortgage, your business?)
  • What's your monthly budget?
  • Do you have any existing coverage?
  • Any health history I should know about for underwriting?

That's it. I take notes and do the homework for you.

📞 Call Now — (574) 392-0192
2
Step Two

I Shop Carriers So You Don't Have To

As an independent agent, I'm appointed with multiple top-rated carriers — Mutual of Omaha, Foresters Financial, Transamerica, North American, and others. I run your information through each one and come back to you with the best options.

What I compare:
  • Monthly premium rates
  • Coverage amounts
  • Approval likelihood
  • Carrier financial strength
What you get:
  • 2–3 clear recommendations
  • Honest pros and cons
  • My personal recommendation
  • Zero pressure to decide
3
Step Three

You Choose — I Handle the Paperwork

When you're ready to move forward, I handle the application entirely. Everything is done over the phone or digitally — whichever you're comfortable with. I'll walk you through every field step by step. No fax machines, no confusing paperwork. If you prefer, I can read the application to you and handle it all while we're on the call.

For simplified issue policies, approval can come back in minutes. For fully underwritten policies, it typically takes 1–3 weeks.

✓
You're Covered

Your Family Is Protected. I'm Still Here.

Once your policy is issued, you receive your documents and I send you a welcome summary that explains exactly what you have, who your beneficiaries are, and how to file a claim.

My job doesn't end at the sale. Life changes — new home, new baby, raise at work — and I'm here to make sure your coverage keeps up. Call me anytime.
