My Story

Why I Started TrueForge

I got into insurance because I saw firsthand what happens when families aren't protected. A single unexpected event — a death, a disability, a serious illness — can wipe out everything a family spent decades building.

What frustrated me was how complicated the industry made it. Medical exams, long approval windows, pushy agents who prioritized commissions over clients. I knew there was a better way.

TrueForge Insurance is built on three commitments: transparency in every conversation, speed in getting you covered, and honesty about what you actually need vs. what sounds good on paper.

When you call me, you're not talking to a call center. You're talking to me — your agent, your advocate, and someone who genuinely cares whether your family is taken care of.
