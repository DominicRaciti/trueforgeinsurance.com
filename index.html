How Coverage Approval Works

1
You answer a short health questionnaire

10–20 yes/no questions over the phone. No doctor visit. No blood draw. Dominic reads every question out loud if you prefer.

2
The carrier checks your prescription history

They verify your answers against a prescription database and the MIB — a standard industry database. This happens automatically and takes minutes.

3
You get an approval decision — often same day

Simplified issue policies are frequently approved the same day or within 24 hours. Guaranteed issue policies are approved on the spot — no health questions at all.

4
Your policy is issued — your family is protected

Your policy documents arrive digitally or by mail. Coverage begins on the policy issue date. Dominic stays on as your point of contact for the life of your policy.

Been declined before? A declined application with one carrier doesn't disqualify you everywhere. Different carriers have different underwriting guidelines — I know which ones are most likely to say yes to your specific health profile.
